# Analysis of Self-Pruning Neural Network

## Observations

- Increasing lambda increases sparsity in the model
- Higher sparsity leads to reduced accuracy
- Trade-off between model efficiency and performance

## Insights

- L1 regularization pushes gate values toward zero
- This results in automatic pruning of weights
- Model learns compact representation during training

## Conclusion

The approach successfully demonstrates dynamic pruning and highlights the importance of balancing sparsity and accuracy.
